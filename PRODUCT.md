# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need quick, focused utilities for everyday media, document, image,
planning, and link tasks. They arrive with a specific job to complete and expect
the interface to stay out of the way.

## Product Purpose

Foundry is an extensible directory of practical web tools. Success means a
user can find the right tool quickly, understand its constraints, complete the
task safely, and retrieve the result without learning a new interaction model.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, and quietly confident. The product should feel like a modern
digital workshop: structured, dependable, and built for the task at hand.

## Anti-references

Avoid glossy SaaS dashboards, floating rounded card collections, decorative
effects, and layouts that only work for the current number of tools. Avoid visual
choices that obscure labels, state, or hierarchy.

## Design Principles

- Make the active task immediately obvious.
- Use a consistent tool contract so new utilities feel familiar.
- Keep state, limits, and system feedback visible in plain language.
- Prefer structural clarity over decoration.
- Preserve Foundry's distinctive technical character without slowing users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, persistent form labels, visible focus states,
44-by-44-pixel minimum targets, textual status alongside color, readable contrast,
screen-reader announcements for asynchronous work, and reduced-motion preferences.
